Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Data controller

The controller is Matchball, Martin Mucha, Jilemnického 1142/8, 040 01 Košice, Slovakia, company ID 57 799 032, contact matchball.app@gmail.com. We process your personal data in accordance with the GDPR (EU) 2016/679.

What we collect

Name, email, date of birth, gender, country, profile photo (or chosen avatar) and details of your bookings, reviews and messages. For coaches also city, pricing, years of experience, licence number and training location. We do not collect phone numbers.

Purpose

We use your data to connect players with coaches, process payments, enable communication, keep the service safe and meet our legal obligations.

Location

If you allow it, we use your approximate device location to show nearby coaches and local weather. We do not store your location or track you in the background. You can revoke the permission in your phone settings at any time.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send you training reminders and messages. They are delivered by Expo via the Apple Push Notification service.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe. Card details never reach us — Stripe stores them. Coaches complete identity verification (KYC) directly with Stripe.

Tax reporting (DAC7)

As a platform we are required by the DAC7 directive to report coaches’ identification and tax details, along with their Matchball earnings, to the tax authority once a year. This data is kept separate from the public profile and concerns coaches only.

Who we share with

Supabase (database and files, EU), Stripe (payments), Expo (notifications), Sentry (crash reports — we send only an account identifier, never your name or email), Google (sign-in, if you use it, and automated screening of your profile photo for inappropriate content), Apple (sign-in, if you use it, and weather forecasts — only the coordinates of the training location are sent), the Slovak register of individuals in sport (when a coach verifies a coaching licence, the IDFO identifier they entered is sent there) and the tax authority as required by DAC7. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account data until you delete your account. Payment records and tax data for 10 years, as accounting law requires. Messages and reviews are deleted together with the account.

Age

Matchball is for people aged 16 and over. A parent books training for a child from their own account.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to restrict processing and to object. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → at the bottom → Delete account).

Complaints

If you believe we handle your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Slovak Data Protection Authority (dataprotection.gov.sk) or the authority in your country of residence.

Contact

For privacy questions contact us at matchball.app@gmail.com.
